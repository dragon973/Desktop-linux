Linux Desktop Environments

GNOME

The standard modern desktop environment focused on simplicity, clean layout, and fluid gesture control.

  • ✨ Modern & Minimalist UI
  • 🚀 Touchpad Gestures
  • ⚙️ Extensible via Plugins
Official Website

KDE Plasma

The king of customization. Gives you absolute freedom to tweak every single detail on your desktop screen.

  • 🎨 Endless Customization
  • ⚡ Highly Optimized & Fast
  • 🔧 Advanced Developer Tools
Official Website

XFCE

Lightning fast, light on resources, highly stable, and built specifically for performance and older hardware.

  • 🪶 Extremely Low RAM/CPU Usage
  • 🛠️ Stable Traditional Layout
  • 🔋 Battery Friendly
Official Website

Cinnamon

The flagship of Linux Mint. Offers a comfortable, traditional Windows-like environment combined with modern power.

  • 🪟 Familiar Start Menu & Taskbar
  • 🧩 Easy Theme Integration
  • ⚖️ Balanced Performance & Looks
Official Website

MATE

The official continuation of classic GNOME 2. Perfect for users who love traditional and clutter-free interfaces.

  • 🕰️ Classic GNOME 2 Design
  • 💻 Low System Footprint
  • 💡 No Distractions
Official Website

LXQt

The ultra-lightweight merger of LXDE and Qt technologies built for minimal resource consumption.

  • 🪶 Ultra Lightweight
  • 🚀 Highly Responsive
  • 🛠️ Modern Qt Framework
Official Website
